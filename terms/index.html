What IPO Saathi is

IPO Saathi is an informational tool for following Indian IPOs (dates, price bands, lot sizes, grey market premium, subscription and listing figures) and for checking your own allotment status by sending a PAN to the registrar that handles the IPO.

It is not a broker. It does not take IPO applications, hold funds or place orders, and it is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst. See the Disclaimer.
