Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026. Applies to the IPO Saathi app for Android and to this website.

In short

  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, phone number or email, so we do not know who you are.
  • Your PANs, nicknames and allotment results stay on your phone. A PAN goes to the registrar's own lookup when you tap Check, and nowhere else.
  • We count how the app is used (screens, taps) under an anonymous install code, using our own server, Mixpanel, Google Firebase and Microsoft Clarity.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, nothing is sold. You can delete your PANs in the app and ask us to delete your usage data.

Who we are

IPO Saathi is an independent app made in India ("we", "us"). This policy covers the IPO Saathi Android app and the website iposaathi.com. For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we decide why and how the usage data described below is processed.

What stays on your phone

These are saved in your phone's secure storage and are never sent to us:

  • the PANs you add, the nickname you give each one, and the holder name a registrar returns;
  • the allotment results of the checks you ran;
  • your theme choice and your reminder settings.

When you tap Check allotment, the app sends the PAN straight from your phone, over HTTPS, to the registrar that handles that IPO (KFin Technologies, MUFG Intime (Link Intime), Bigshare Services or Maashitla Securities). It is the same lookup you would do on the registrar's own website. We do not see the request or the result. From that point the registrar's own privacy policy applies.

We do not sell or rent data, we do not show ads, and we do not share data for advertising. We may disclose data if a law or a court order requires it.

Your data is processed in India (our database) and, through the services above, in the United States and other countries.

What we do not collect

No name, email address or phone number. No location. No contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. No advertising ID. The only permission the app asks for is to show notifications.

This website sets no cookies and uses no third-party fonts. It loads Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps (in its cookie-free mode), counts visits and button clicks with Mixpanel, using a random code that is forgotten when you close the tab, and it sends the contact form on the Support page only when you press Send.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you remove a PAN, delete all PANs or uninstall the app.
  • Usage events in our database: 180 days, then they are deleted automatically.
  • Contact form messages: up to a year in our database, then deleted automatically. The copy in our inbox stays until we delete it.
  • Push tokens: until notifications stop working for that phone, or you ask us to delete it.
  • Mixpanel, Google and Microsoft: as set in each service's own retention settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Remove a PAN: in the Allotment tab, or choose Profile → Delete all saved PANs. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.
  • Reminders: switch each type off in Profile → Notifications, or in Android's notification settings.
  • Usage data: the app has no switch to turn it off yet. To have it deleted, open Profile → Feedback, write that you want your usage data deleted and send. The message carries your support code, which is how we find your data. We delete it from our database and ask the analytics services to delete theirs where they allow it, and we reply within 30 days. Uninstalling stops new data from being sent.
  • Your rights: under the DPDP Act you can ask what personal data we hold, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and complain to us. Write to the address below.

Security

PANs are kept in the phone's secure storage, which is backed by Android's Keystore. Everything the app sends goes over HTTPS. Our server never receives a PAN, so there is no list of PANs for anyone to breach. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that it is.

Children

IPO Saathi is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If the app starts to collect something new, we will update this page and its date before the change reaches you, and tell you in the app when the change is significant. The version on this page is always the current one.
