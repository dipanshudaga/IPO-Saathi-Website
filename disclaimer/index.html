The app does not rate IPOs or tell you what to do. What you do with the information is your decision, and you may wish to speak to a qualified, registered adviser first.

About GMP

The grey market premium is an unofficial figure from informal trading. It is not issued, checked or endorsed by any exchange or regulator, it can differ a lot from the actual listing price, and it can be missing for an IPO. A high or rising GMP does not mean an IPO will list at a gain.

Data can be late or wrong

The numbers come from third-party sources and are collected automatically. They can be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and anything we cannot find shows as N/A. Allotment results come from the registrar's own system. Before you act on a date, a price or a result, confirm it with the exchange, the registrar or the official offer document. See Data sources.
