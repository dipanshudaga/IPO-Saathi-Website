Common questions

Is IPO Saathi free?

Yes. There are no ads and no sign-up, and there is nothing to buy.

Where is my PAN stored?

Only on your phone, in its secure storage. It goes to the registrar's own lookup when you tap Check, and nowhere else. It never reaches our servers. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I add a PAN?

Open the Allotment tab and tap the plus button. Give each PAN a nickname, such as Mummy or Papa, so you can tell them apart.

How do I remove a PAN?

In the Allotment tab, tap the remove icon next to the PAN and confirm. To remove all of them at once, go to Profile and choose Delete all saved PANs. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Which registrars does it check?

KFin Technologies, MUFG Intime (Link Intime), Bigshare Services and Maashitla Securities are checked for you. For any other registrar, you can set the status yourself.

Why does it say N/A?

The source has not published that figure yet, or we could not match it with confidence. For a new IPO this is normal in the first hours. See Data sources.

What is GMP?

The grey market premium is an unofficial indication of what traders in the grey market are paying above an IPO's issue price. It is not issued by any exchange and can differ from the real listing price. See the Disclaimer.

How do I switch reminders off?

Go to Profile, then Notifications, and turn each type off. You can also turn notifications off for IPO Saathi in Android's settings.

Does IPO Saathi take my IPO application or money?

No. It only shows information and checks allotment. You apply through your own broker or bank.

How do I get my usage data deleted?

Go to Profile, then Feedback, write that you want your usage data deleted and send. The message carries your support code, which is how we find your data. We reply within 30 days. More in the Privacy Policy.
